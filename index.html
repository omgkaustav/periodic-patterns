The unit is the tile

How neighboring copies attach — not a 4D surface.

square: 4 sides, two pairs of opposite edges hexagon: 6 sides, three pairs of opposite edges

The dropdown is only the wallpaper rule: how the next copy of the tile is placed so the simulation’s neighbors wrap. Translate means a plain copy. Flip means that copy is mirrored before it attaches. Rotate 180° and the mirror modes are the other ways a square or hexagon can meet itself. Matching edge pairs share a color; arrowheads on those edges show whether the identification preserves orientation or reverses it. The chemistry never leaves the tile; it just sees those copies as its periodic boundary.
